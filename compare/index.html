HOA Fee Comparison Matrix

Not all housing associations are created equal. See how fees vary across different property types in 2026.

Fee Ranges Across Property Types

  • Single Family Home: $150 - $400/mo. Typically lowest fees as homeowner handles all individual home exteriors, roofs, and yards personally.
  • Condominium Unit: $300 - $800+/mo. Higher relative dues because boards manage and insure the complete vertical building walls and roofs collectively.
  • Townhouse Property: $200 - $500/mo. Shared exterior walls and roofs lead to moderate bulk maintenance structures.
