Understanding the Real Expense of Association Living

Many first-time homebuyers assume that their mortgage payment is the only monthly carrying cost. However, choosing a property with a homeowners association adds a mandatory recurring obligation that typically increases over time. HOA fees are legally binding and non-negotiable carrying costs used to preserve shared infrastructure, exterior siding, roofs, and central community amenities like swimming pools, tennis courts, and safety gates.

Before purchasing, it is critical to perform due diligence. We recommend analyzing the association's general reserve study, checking past assessment records, and assessing whether self-management is cheaper. For instance, self-managing your own private pool typically adds $150–250 per month, while private lawn care averages $120–200 per month. If your condo or townhouse association dues consolidate these expenses, they may provide a significant bulk discount over individual management.
