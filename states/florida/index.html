Important Florida HOA Laws & Mandates

Before closing on any Florida condo or home, understand these crucial legislative mandates:

  • Senate Bill 4-D: Mandates detailed Structural Integrity Reserve Studies (SIRS) for buildings over 3 stories.
  • Zero Waiver Rule: Boards are legally prohibited from underfunding or waiving reserve accounts for vital components.
  • Transfer Disclosures: Explicit transfer documents are required before any deed registers a closing.
