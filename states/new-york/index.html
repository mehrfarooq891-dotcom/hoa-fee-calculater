Important New York HOA & Co-op Guidelines

In New York, boards carry exceptional regulatory and selection powers:

  • Board Interview Approvals: Condo and co-op boards operate with enormous authority, including interviewing and rejecting prospective buyers.
  • Financial Disclosures: State laws require boards to provide fully audited financial ledgers.
  • Foreclosure Powers: Continuous non-payment of assessments can trigger rapid statutory foreclosures.
